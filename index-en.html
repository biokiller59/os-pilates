FAQ

Before your first visit

I've never done Pilates. Is it for me?

Yes. In your first class the instructor will ask how you feel and about any injuries, and offer easier versions of the exercises. Mat or Healthy Back is the easiest place to start.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes and socks, ideally grip socks. We provide mats, towels and water. Grip socks are also sold at the front desk.

Can I practice with back pain?

In most cases, yes, and it helps, but check with your doctor first. Let your instructor know before class so they can keep the workload safe.

What if I can't make it?

Cancel 8 hours ahead or earlier for free. Late cancellations count against your pass, since no one else can take the spot in time.
